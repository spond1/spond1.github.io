## The short version

I’m currently pursuing an MBA at UC Berkeley Haas after five years in economic consulting. Before business school, I worked with clients and teams on financial strategy, transaction analysis, regulatory questions, and high-stakes reporting.

I care about the last mile of analysis: whether a model is clear enough to trust, whether a recommendation can survive contact with reality, and whether the people responsible for acting on it have what they need.
