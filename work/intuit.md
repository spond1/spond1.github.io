## Context

Mid-market data was fragmented. The team needed a clear, shared view of one of Intuit’s big bets.

## Contribution

I consolidated the data into a single plan book and dashboard for audiences ranging from analysts through SVPs. Using Cursor and Claude Code, I automated roughly 70% of the manual preparation.

## Result

The work created a trusted read on one of Intuit’s big bets. The plan book and dashboard connected the underlying analysis to a view leaders and analysts could use.
