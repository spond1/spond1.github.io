## Context

Atomery is a venture developing affordable housing for students and low-income communities in Phnom Penh.

## Contribution

I designed and implemented a university student housing model offering $1/day accommodations, and established mentorship and scholarship programs. I also co-led structural innovations in city housing and co-managed more than 80 units.

## Result

The student housing model reduced living costs by over 50% for 30 students. The co-managed units generated 80M riel—approximately $20K—in monthly revenue.
