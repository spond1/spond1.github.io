## Context

For a private-equity client, reporting and audit workflows involved five analysts, seven expert teams, and attorneys.

## Contribution

I coordinated the teams to redesign the workflows, scripted audits, and built dynamic templates for client-facing reports.

## Result

The updated workflows cut report turnaround by 40 days and improved efficiency by 50%.

## Selected financial strategy work

- Constructed scenario-based financial models for a major NBA team, testing valuation approaches and substantiating an $80M insurance claim through a “but-for” analysis.
- Led a team of seven revising utility pricing strategy, turning a static one-year model into a 35-year framework and integrating CapEx to guide multi-billion-dollar capital planning and new debt financing.
- Evaluated WeWork’s joint venture due diligence in the $3B SoftBank tender offer, benchmarking against industry standards to assess transaction risks and inform strategic recommendations.

## Project and firm leadership

- Collaborated with senior leadership to secure a 16-analyst team for a high-priority DOJ offshore tax scheme triangulation project contributing roughly 5% of firm revenue.
- Identified and pitched workstreams that increased annual client fees from $300K to $1M.
- Led Excel training for roughly 50 new hires annually and modernized firm-wide templates to standardize branding and strengthen client-facing deliverables.
- Pioneered Brattle’s five-year sponsorship of the Out for Undergrad conference and its first three industry networking events.
- Chaired BProud, growing membership from 25 to 60 and introducing mentorship programs and the firm’s inaugural panel series.
